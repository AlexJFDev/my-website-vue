# Getting Organized
Unfortunately, another late post. The last two weeks have been especially busy with rush week and then stuff with my new frat.
This week, I wanted to write about how I (try) to keep organized.
## Time
Managing your time properly is probably the most important way to keep organized. I manage my time with a google calendar.
**My Google Calendar**
![My Google Calendar](https://i.imgur.com/BmHclCg.png "My Google Calendar")
I try to use 3 main calendars, the daily calendar, the weekly calendar, and the monthly calendar. The daily calendar is updated once a day, weekly once a week, and monthly once a month. In practice, I only use the daily calendar regularly. Most things that would make sense to put on the other two are recurring events which I put into different calendars.
Each calendar also has a number at the start of its name. Google calendar sorts your calendars alphabetically and there is no alternative. Using the numbers is my way of circumventing that limitation.
## Files
I try to organize my files around a central folder. On systems with a UI; I use the desktop, on systems without a UI; I usually use the user folder, and in Google Drive I use the root folder. I try to keep these top level folders free of files and have a folder for everything. I am good about this rule everywhere except Google Drive.
I also follow the Capitalized-Kebab-Case naming convention for my files except when I'm using something, like Java or Python, which has a different standard. This is because this naming convention is rarely used by the software and programming languages that I am familiar with so it easily lets me differentiate between what is usually code and documents, photos, or something else.
